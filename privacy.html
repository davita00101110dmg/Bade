Privacy Policy

Bade · last updated 22 August 2026

Bade collects nothing about you. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server that belongs to Bade. This policy explains what that means in practice, and describes the one network request the app can make.

Your bank statement

When you import a statement, Bade reads it in memory on your device and discards it immediately. The file is never copied, never written to storage by Bade, and never transmitted anywhere. Bade does not keep the statement after it has read it.

Bade never asks for your online banking password, card numbers, or any credential for your bank. It cannot connect to your bank, and it has no way to do so.

What is stored on your device

Once you confirm what Bade found, it saves the subscriptions you chose — the merchant name, amount, currency, how often it charges, and the dates it has charged — together with any exchange rates your statement printed. This is stored only on your device. It is not synced to iCloud and it is not sent anywhere.

Reminders, totals and the home-screen widget are all calculated on your device from that stored data.

The one network request

Bade can ask the National Bank of Georgia for a day's published exchange rates. This happens only when a statement did not print a rate that Bade needs, and you can switch it off entirely in Settings.

The request is a plain GET to the National Bank's public rates endpoint, and the only thing it carries is a date:

  • No account details, no card details, no personal identifier
  • No merchant names, and no transaction of any kind
  • Not even which currency you hold — Bade asks for the whole day's rates rather than a specific currency, so the request cannot reveal what you are subscribed to

Bade sends nothing else, to anyone, ever.

Analytics, tracking and advertising

There are none. Bade contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party tracking software of any kind. Nothing you do in the app is recorded or reported. Bade does not track you across apps or websites.

Purchases

Bade Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple. Bade never sees your payment details. It only asks Apple whether a purchase exists on your Apple Account.

Deleting your data

Open Settings in Bade and choose Delete everything, which removes every subscription and exchange rate Bade has stored. Deleting the app removes all of it as well. Because nothing was ever sent anywhere, there is nothing elsewhere to delete.

Children

Bade is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
