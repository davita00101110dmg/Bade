Bade — Support

Bade finds every recurring charge in your bank statement, on your phone.

Getting your statement into Bade

  1. Open your bank's app and go to Accounts
  2. Choose Statement, and set the period to the last 12 months
  3. Choose PDF as the format
  4. Share it, and pick Bade

A longer statement gives Bade more to work with. Three months is the minimum worth trying; a year is much better, because a yearly subscription only shows up once.

Which statements can Bade read?

Bade reads statements exported from Bank of Georgia and TBC. Statement reading is in beta: a bank can change its export format without warning, and when that happens Bade needs updating. If a statement will not read, please get in touch — that is the most useful thing you can tell us.

Bade found fewer subscriptions than I expected

On the review screen, only the charges Bade is certain about arrive already ticked. Anything below them is a question rather than a rejection — Bade saw the charges but could not prove a rhythm. Tick anything you recognise before confirming.

A yearly subscription needs a statement long enough to contain it, or a merchant Bade already knows, before it can be found at all.

Nothing leaves your phone

Your statement is read in memory and discarded. Bade has no account, no server, and never asks for your bank password. The only thing it ever sends is a request to the National Bank of Georgia for a day's exchange rates, and you can switch that off in Settings. See the privacy policy.

Bade Pro

A one-time purchase, not a subscription. It adds renewal reminders, a calendar of upcoming charges, the exchange rate your bank really used on foreign charges, export to CSV or JSON, and a home-screen widget.

If you have bought Pro and it is not showing — for example on a new phone — open Settings in Bade, tap Bade Pro, and choose Restore a purchase. It is tied to your Apple Account, so there is nothing to pay again.

Reminders are not arriving

Check that notifications are allowed for Bade in iOS Settings → Notifications → Bade, and that a reminder time is set in Bade's own Settings. Reminders are part of Bade Pro.
